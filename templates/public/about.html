About VentureBridge

VentureBridge exists because anyone can list a "startup" on the internet, but very few platforms prove that the people and companies behind a listing are who they claim to be. We built VentureBridge around one idea: trust is the product.

Founders upload real legal documents. Individuals verify their identity with live-captured photos. Admins review every submission. Only then do badges appear — so when an Investor or Job Seeker acts on what they see, there's a real verification trail behind it.

Funding introductions on VentureBridge are structured as milestone-based agreements, not lump-sum transfers — funds are released in stages as founders hit agreed milestones, with an admin releasing each stage and a small platform fee applied along the way.
